Changes: source components selected, welded, reduced, registered together,
rescaled, registered to eye/chin/crown landmarks and cage-fitted to an adult game head; closed skull cutaway generated
in Blender; normals and vertex ambient occlusion generated; custom XRA export.
28 teeth from the IS-A archive were fitted using the same registration and
cage as the skull, reduced into two arches, and cleaned of tiny disconnected
fragments. The reveal's tissue shading and edge treatment are procedural.
The combined anatomy asset and editable Blender scene are distributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0, with the original BodyParts3D attribution retained.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ This applies
to anatomy assets, not to the independent plugin code, which is GPLv3.

The ethmoid and third molars remain absent. These meshes
are adapted from different donors and are an artistic fit, not a medical model.
No Sniper Elite assets, restricted Nexus assets or Bethesda meshes are included.

The neck uses BodyParts3D atlas, axis and third to seventh cervical
vertebrae, hyoid and trachea (part-of archive) and thyroid and cricoid
cartilage (is-a archive), registered with the same transform as the skull.
BodyParts3D has no cervical spinal cord, so the plugin's build generates a
tube that follows the vertebral canal measured from those donors.

The torso uses BodyParts3D first to twelfth thoracic and first to fifth
lumbar vertebrae, sacrum, hip bones, first to twelfth ribs, first to seventh
costal cartilages, manubrium, body of sternum, xiphoid process, clavicles,
scapulae, heart, liver, stomach, duodenum, jejunum, ileum, large intestine
and kidneys (part-of archive), registered into an adult game body by its hip
and shoulder joints and its skin profile, cage-fitted under that skin,
reduced, and closed by voxel remeshing where the donor parts are many
pieces. BodyParts3D models the lungs only as airway and vessel trees, so
each lung is grown from the hull of its own trees with the heart, liver and
stomach carved out; the spinal cord is a generated tube through the measured
vertebral canal. The femoral and humeral heads are used only to locate the
hip and shoulder joints and the donor skin only to measure the body's
profile; neither is distributed.

The legs use BodyParts3D femur, patella, tibia, fibula, tarsal, metatarsal
and phalanx bones of both sides (part-of archive), each segment registered
onto an adult game leg by its hip, knee, ankle and ball of the foot, cage-
fitted under that skin and reduced; the foot bones of each side are joined
into one simplified foot.

The arms use BodyParts3D humerus, radius, ulna, carpal and metacarpal bones
of both sides (part-of archive), each segment registered onto an adult game
arm by its shoulder, elbow, wrist and knuckle, the radius turned about its
own pronation axis to the game's hand, cage-fitted under that skin and
reduced; the carpals and metacarpals of each side are joined into one
simplified hand.

The men's torso uses BodyParts3D left and right testis, epididymis, corpus
cavernosum and corpus spongiosum of penis and glans penis (is-a archive),
anchored at the registered pubic symphysis at the torso's scale and fitted
inside an adult game body's crotch. Women's torso leaves them out.

Horses use "Horse Skeleton" by Diego Luján García, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0
International:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-skeleton-eaca504567604e879b8ab2cf2763025e
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: split into its bones and fitted into the HAP horse's body, the
trunk scaled into its barrel, the skull squashed into its head, the neck
laid along its neck bones and each leg bone re-posed onto its leg and
stretched or shortened to its joints, then pulled under its skin where it
poked through. The tail past the croup is left out, and the gaps in the
skull and two rib cartilages are closed by voxel remeshing. The skull's
braincase is carved to hold the brain, opening out of the back of the skull
along the brainstem.

The horse's organs are three scans, each licensed under CC Attribution 4.0
International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
"Horse brain" by jc196643
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-brain-f2bb07bb7778424d850c8be09d96bbc8
"Heart (horse) Plastinated" by the University of Sarajevo Veterinary Faculty
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/heart-horse-plastinated-fce338c614f44b5aae3ddd38226b97b7
"Equine GI Tract" by auburton2021
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/equine-gi-tract-3692febcfa34423d9bd9b598f4fe8d04
Changes: each scan closed by voxel remeshing (the GI tract first wrapped
over its holes by an octree remesh), its largest piece kept and reduced,
then turned, scaled and placed in the horse: the brain in the skull, the
heart low in the chest between the fifth and sixth ribs, and the GI tract
in the belly behind it.

The HAP horse's own body and skeleton are used only for the fit and are not
distributed. The adapted horse anatomy is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0
like the rest, with the original attributions retained.

Fractures are carved at runtime from the donor skull, teeth and brain
themselves, so every fragment retains the anatomy attribution above. The
flying lumps of torn brain are procedurally generated in the plugin.
